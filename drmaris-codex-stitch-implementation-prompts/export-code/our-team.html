SURGICAL PLANNING

Frequently Asked Questions About Plastic Surgery in Vietnam

Is DR. MARIS AESTHETICS a spa or cosmetic surgery provider?

We are a specialized cosmetic surgery provider. Unlike spas or beauty clinics, all surgical procedures are performed in a fully accredited international hospital (CIH) with comprehensive medical infrastructure.

Who performs my cosmetic surgery?

Every surgery is personally performed by Dr. Maris (Dr. Tran Minh Huy). We do not use 'ghost surgeons' or junior residents; your care is managed directly by the lead surgeon from planning to follow-up.

Can I speak with Dr. Maris before travelling to Vietnam?

Yes. We require an online consultation via video call to review your medical history, photographs, and surgical goals before any travel arrangements are finalized.

Does Dr. Maris perform revision plastic surgery?

Dr. Maris specializes in complex revision cases, including corrective rhinoplasty, breast implant revision, and scar management for patients who had unsatisfactory results elsewhere.

Can failed cosmetic surgery always be corrected?

While many issues can be significantly improved, correction depends on the amount of healthy tissue remaining and the nature of the previous surgery. A thorough assessment is required to determine realistic outcomes.

How long should I stay in Vietnam after plastic surgery?

Stay duration varies by procedure, typically ranging from 7 to 14 days. This ensures you are past the immediate post-operative phase and have had your initial follow-up examinations before flying.

How much does plastic surgery cost in Vietnam?

Costs are significantly more competitive than in Australia or the US, but we prioritize safety and hospital-based care over 'budget' pricing. Quotes are provided after your clinical assessment.

What should I send if I need revision surgery?

Please provide your previous operative reports, the date of your last surgery, high-resolution photos of the area, and a detailed description of your specific concerns.

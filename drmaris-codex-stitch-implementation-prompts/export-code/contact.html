Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a fee for the initial consultation? expand_more

Yes, we charge a nominal fee for the specialist's time, which is fully credited toward any treatment you choose to proceed with.

How soon can I get an appointment? expand_more

Typically, we can accommodate new patient consultations within 3-5 business days depending on physician availability.

Do you offer virtual consultations? expand_more

For international patients or initial skin assessments, we offer secure video consultations via our patient portal.

How should I prepare for my consultation? expand_more

Please bring a list of your current skincare products, any medical history relevant to your visit, and arrive with a clean face if you're discussing facial treatments.

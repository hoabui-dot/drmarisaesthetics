# SECTION 02 — UNDERSTANDING BUTTOCK AUGMENTATION

## Component Type

Educational / procedure overview section

## Heading

Understanding Buttock Augmentation

## Content

Buttock augmentation is cosmetic surgery intended to change the:

- volume
- shape
- projection

of the buttocks.

Two principal approaches are relevant to this DR. MARIS service page:

1. Buttock Augmentation – Fat Transfer (BBL)
2. Buttock Augmentation – Implants

The American Society of Plastic Surgeons describes buttock augmentation as surgery that may use either implants or transfer of the patient's own fat.

The appropriate approach can be influenced by:

- existing buttock shape
- available donor fat
- goals
- activity level
- skin quality
- skin elasticity

## Important Distinction

Buttock augmentation should not be confused with a buttock lift.

A buttock lift primarily removes or repositions loose tissue rather than simply adding volume.

## Core Consultation Question

The consultation should not begin with:

> "Which procedure gives the biggest buttocks?"

It should begin with:

> "What anatomical change is appropriate for your body, and which surgical method can reasonably achieve it?"

# SECTION 03 — BUTTOCK AUGMENTATION WITH FAT TRANSFER — BBL

## Component Type

Primary procedure detail section

## Heading

Buttock Augmentation with Fat Transfer — BBL

## Content

A Brazilian Butt Lift, commonly shortened to BBL, uses autologous fat transfer.

"Autologous" means the fat comes from the patient's own body.

The procedure generally involves three stages:

1. Liposuction of donor areas
2. Fat processing
3. Fat transfer to the buttocks

## Core Principle

BBL is not only a buttock procedure.

Because donor fat is collected through liposuction, the surgical plan may also affect the contour of:

- waist
- abdomen
- back
- thighs
- hips

depending on where suitable donor fat is available.

# SECTION 04 — HOW BBL WORKS

## Component Type

Procedure process section

## Heading

How BBL Fat Transfer Works

### Stage 1 — Liposuction of Donor Areas

Fat is collected through liposuction from areas where suitable donor fat is available.

Possible donor areas depend on individual anatomy and may include areas such as:

- abdomen
- waist
- back
- thighs

Liposuction also affects overall body contour.

Planning should therefore consider the relationship between:

- waist
- hips
- buttocks

rather than viewing the buttocks in isolation.

---

### Stage 2 — Fat Processing

The removed material is prepared so that suitable fat can be used for grafting.

---

### Stage 3 — Fat Transfer to the Buttocks

Prepared fat is placed into selected areas to modify:

- contour
- projection

ASPS describes BBL as fat obtained by liposuction, processed and then injected into the buttocks using surgical cannulas.

# SECTION 05 — WHO MAY CONSIDER A BBL?

## Component Type

Candidate / indications section

## Heading

Who May Consider a BBL?

## Intro

Fat-transfer buttock augmentation may be considered when a patient:

- wants greater buttock volume or projection
- wants selected contour changes
- has sufficient suitable donor fat
- is medically appropriate for liposuction and fat grafting
- understands that not all transferred fat will necessarily survive
- accepts that results depend on anatomy and healing
- has realistic expectations

## Important Limitation

A BBL cannot simply be scaled to any desired volume.

The amount that can appropriately be transferred depends on factors including:

- donor-fat availability
- recipient-tissue capacity
- surgical safety
- patient's anatomy

## Limited Donor Fat

Patients with very little suitable donor fat may need to discuss whether an implant-based approach is more appropriate.

## Core Message

Desired size alone should not determine the surgical plan.

# SECTION 06 — BUTTOCK IMPLANTS

## Component Type

Primary procedure detail section

## Heading

Buttock Implants

## Content

Buttock augmentation can also be performed using implants specifically designed for gluteal augmentation.

Unlike a BBL, this procedure does not depend on harvesting a large amount of fat from another part of the body.

ASPS describes buttock implant surgery as placement of silicone implants through surgically planned incisions.

Implant position and surgical approach depend on:

- anatomy
- implant selection
- surgeon judgment

## When Implants May Be Discussed

Implants may be discussed with patients who:

- want increased projection
- have limited appropriate donor fat for fat transfer

## Implant-Specific Considerations

Implant surgery introduces considerations including:

- surgical incision
- scar
- implant positioning
- infection risk
- wound healing
- implant migration or displacement
- implant rupture
- tissue pressure
- possible future revision
- possible implant removal

## Important Verification Rule

Do not publish unverified details about:

- implant brand
- implant shape
- implant size
- exact implant placement technique

until these details are confirmed by DR. MARIS.

# SECTION 10 — BBL SAFETY REQUIRES SPECIAL ATTENTION

## Component Type

High-priority safety section

## Heading

BBL Safety Requires Special Attention

## Priority

HIGH

This section should be visually prominent and should not be reduced to a small disclaimer.

## Content

A BBL deserves a more detailed safety discussion than ordinary marketing pages often provide.

Multi-society guidance involving ASPS, The Aesthetic Society and other plastic-surgery organizations has recognized gluteal fat grafting as carrying significant risk compared with many other elective aesthetic procedures.

A particularly serious risk is fat entering large blood vessels, which can cause a potentially fatal fat embolism.

## Current General Safety Guidance

Current safety guidance emphasizes keeping transferred fat in the appropriate:

**subcutaneous plane above the gluteal muscle fascia**

Multi-society safety guidance also supports real-time ultrasound guidance as a way of helping surgeons verify the anatomical plane during fat injection.

Recent ASPS patient-safety guidance encourages patients considering BBL to discuss:

- surgeon's injection plane
- surgeon's experience
- use of ultrasound guidance

## Critical DR. MARIS Content Rule

Do not claim that DR. MARIS:

- uses ultrasound guidance
- injects only in a specific anatomical plane
- uses a particular cannula
- follows a particular proprietary BBL technique

unless the exact protocol has been verified.

## Core Message

BBL safety information must be transparent and must not be replaced by purely aesthetic marketing claims.

## Recovery After BBL

Both areas need to heal:

1. liposuction donor areas
2. buttocks receiving transferred fat

ASPS advises that prolonged direct pressure on newly transferred fat is commonly restricted during early recovery.

Patients may be instructed to:

- sleep on their side
- sleep on their abdomen
- use modified sitting positions

Specific timing varies between surgeons and patients.

### FAQ 3 — What is the difference between a BBL and buttock implants?

A BBL uses fat collected from your own body through liposuction and transfers it to the buttocks.

Buttock implant surgery uses surgically placed gluteal implants and does not depend on having enough donor fat.

Each method has different:

- benefits
- limitations
- recovery considerations
- risks

Dr. Maris should determine the appropriate option after examining your anatomy and goals.

---

### FAQ 4 — What if I do not have enough fat for a BBL?

A BBL requires sufficient suitable donor fat.

Patients with limited donor fat may not be able to achieve the volume they expect through fat transfer alone.

An implant-based approach may be discussed in some cases.

The decision depends on:

- buttock anatomy
- skin quality
- health
- desired projection
- overall surgical assessment

---

### FAQ 5 — Is Brazilian Butt Lift surgery risky?

Yes.

BBL has specific risks that require careful discussion.

Plastic-surgery societies have highlighted gluteal fat grafting because accidental entry of fat into large blood vessels can cause a potentially fatal fat embolism.

Current safety guidance emphasizes appropriate fat-injection planes and supports real-time ultrasound guidance.

Patients should ask their surgeon specifically about the safety technique used.

## Implementation Note

Do not modify this FAQ into marketing language that minimizes the risk.

---

### FAQ 6 — Does all the transferred fat survive after a BBL?

No.

Some transferred fat may be reabsorbed during healing.

This means the early postoperative size is not the same as the final result.

ASPS notes that:

- final results take time to develop
- some initial fat volume is naturally reabsorbed

The amount retained varies between individuals.

A specific final volume should not be guaranteed.

---

### FAQ 7 — Can BBL be combined with 360° liposuction?

Liposuction is inherently part of fat harvesting for BBL.

However, whether extensive 360° abdominal liposuction can safely be included in the same surgical plan depends on:

- patient
- surgical extent
- medical assessment

DR. MARIS publishes a separate price for 360° Abdomen Liposuction.

Therefore, a combined package price should not be assumed from the individual prices.

This should not be interpreted as the exact DR. MARIS protocol.

Dr. Maris should provide procedure-specific instructions after surgery.

# CORE PAGE MESSAGE

Buttock augmentation can be performed using the patient's own fat or surgically placed implants.

A BBL uses fat obtained through liposuction, processed and transferred to selected areas of the buttocks.

Buttock implants provide augmentation without depending on sufficient donor fat but introduce implant-specific surgical and long-term considerations.

Neither approach is automatically superior.

The appropriate method depends on:

- buttock anatomy
- available donor fat
- skin quality
- waist and hip proportions
- desired projection
- previous surgery
- medical condition
- surgical risk

BBL also requires a particularly careful safety discussion because gluteal fat grafting carries specific risks, including potentially serious fat embolism.

Dr. Maris personally assesses the patient, develops the surgical plan, performs the surgery and participates in postoperative follow-up.

The objective is not to create the largest possible buttock size.

The objective is to determine which surgical approach can reasonably address the patient's anatomy and goals while maintaining appropriate attention to surgical safety.

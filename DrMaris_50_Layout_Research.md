## Research method and decision criteria

Evaluated appropriateness for two named hospital environments: (1) both hospitals visible in normal scroll, (2) architecture/image authenticity, (3) clinical credibility and plain-language information, (4) luxury visual feel, (5) sensible mobile/keyboard experience, (6) suitability for purposeful GSAP animation. ## Pattern synthesis

**Strongest reference ideas**: hospital location cards from Mayo Clinic and Cleveland Clinic (instant venue clarity); architectural building-first institution framing from Johns Hopkins; equally weighted destination introductions from Capella/Soneva/Six Senses; trust signals and direct CTAs from Houston Methodist, Providence and local hospital websites.

**Three layouts considered**:
- **A — Twin Equal Cards:** best immediate parity and simplicity; not sufficiently editorial for flagship Dr. Maris visual language.
- **B — Cinematic Editorial Chapters (CHOSEN):** two alternating wide photo/text compositions with smaller overlaid interior images. Hero visual contrast + complete readability + moderate GSAP ScrollTrigger storytelling.
- **C — Pinned Horizontal Carousel:** dramatic but hides one location until interaction, more motion/accessibility/performance risk. Rejected.

**Decision**: B, with 2 mandatory images per hospital, desktop reversal of order, mobile stacked content, no forced scroll. All photos in implementation prompt are temporary external visual references and require licensing/editorial approval.
